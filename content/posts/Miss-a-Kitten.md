After the morning run, I played with my iPhone in the shade of trees.

Suddenly there was a cry in baby’s voice from the flowerbeds. I followed the sound, and saw a lovely kitten sitting on the root of a tree, looking around. The sound in the little  body was higher than a wave. When I was young, I had the talent to learn to call a cat. I then imitated a cat, and the eyes of the little kitten lit up, and approached me carefully, as if she had met her loved one. and followed me to the downstairs of the apartment. She was as small as the palm of mine, and the legs were not long enough when she stepped down.

<!--more-->

I hesitated about taking her home for a long time and finally decided to adopt her. I went upstairs and took a box to put her in. When I went downstairs, the little kitten was missing. I looked for a while and didn't have her trace anywhere, and finally gave it up. I guessed someone should take her away a step ahead, and I couldn't help but lose her. This kind of mood seemed familiar to me.

I have met many girls who I ran into or ran into me in my 28 years of life. Some of them are just like today's little kitten following me, but I hesitated too long to finally miss them.It’s my shortcoming which has led me to easily miss many chances, such as girls, and stocks.

Leave a place,the scenery is no longer belongs to you;miss a person,that person would no longer do with you.
